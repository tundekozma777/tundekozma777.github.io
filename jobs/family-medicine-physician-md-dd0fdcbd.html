Responsibilities

  • Annotate and review clinical content and medical records (office visit notes, assessments and plans, referrals, lab and imaging results, and care coordination documentation), ensuring accuracy, clinical soundness, and completeness.

  • Hands-on clinical experience in primary care or family medicine settings (e.g., clinics, community practices, health systems).
  • Broad clinical exposure across common acute and chronic conditions, preventive care, and multiple patient age groups.
  • Previous annotation or clinical review experience is a plus.
  • Proficiency with both written and verbal communication, with a strong attention to clarity and detail.
