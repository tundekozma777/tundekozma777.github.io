About the Role

Join our customer's team as an Arabic Language Expert (Saudi) and play a pivotal role in enhancing multilingual language resources. This part-time, remote opportunity is ideal for native Saudi Arabic speakers who are passionate about language, cultural authenticity, and effective communication. Utilize your linguistic expertise to deliver high-quality audio samples and precise transcriptions for an impactful language learning experience.

Responsibilities

  • Record a variety of audio phrases in native Saudi Arabic using your own device, contributing to a diverse audio database.
  • Submit clear, accurate transcriptions of all recorded phrases, ensuring linguistic precision and cultural relevance.
  • Collaborate remotely with the customer's team to receive instructions, provide feedback, and deliver completed tasks.
  • Demonstrate professional written and verbal communication skills throughout all team engagements.
  • Maintain deadlines and manage task priorities efficiently in a part-time, flexible work structure.
  • Ensure the authenticity of language usage, reflecting true Saudi dialect and expressions.
  • Promote diversity in recordings by utilizing different microphones or smartphones as available.

Required Qualifications

  • Native proficiency in Saudi Arabic, with a deep understanding of local dialects and cultural nuances.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both Arabic and English.
  • Demonstrated ability to transcribe spoken language accurately and efficiently.
  • Comfort working independently in a remote, part-time environment.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to linguistic quality and consistency.
  • Multilingual abilities, with proficiency in English to follow project guidelines and team communications.
  • Ability to use a range of audio recording devices (no restriction on equipment quality).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience in language teaching, linguistics, or translation roles.
  • Familiarity with language technology or language learning tools.
  • Experience in delivering high-quality audio and written language data on digital platforms.
