Medical Oncologist

Remote, United States only$160–$180 per hourContractor

About the Role

In this role, you'll use your clinical expertise to evaluate AI-generated transcripts and medical notes from outpatient oncology encounters. Your feedback will help improve the accuracy, completeness, and clinical relevance of AI-assisted documentation.

Responsibilities

  • Review AI-generated transcripts of outpatient oncology visits for clinical accuracy, completeness, and clarity.
  • Evaluate clinical documentation, including History of Present Illness (HPI), interval histories, and Assessment and Plans (A&Ps).
  • Identify inaccuracies or missing details related to cancer diagnoses, staging, treatment plans, and laboratory or imaging findings.
  • Assess documentation of systemic cancer therapies, including chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapies, and supportive care.
  • Review how clinical notes capture treatment responses, adverse effects, disease progression, and follow-up care.
  • Provide clear, actionable feedback to improve AI-generated clinical notes and EHR documentation.

Required Qualifications

  • MD, DO, or equivalent medical degree, with board certification or eligibility in Medical Oncology.
  • At least 2 years of recent outpatient medical oncology experience following fellowship.
  • Strong clinical experience diagnosing and managing solid tumors, including treatment planning and longitudinal patient care.
  • Proven ability to independently document HPIs, interval histories, and Assessment and Plans.
  • Solid understanding of systemic cancer therapies, treatment-related toxicities, supportive care, and interpretation of laboratory and imaging results.
  • Strong written communication skills and attention to clinical detail.
  • Based in the United States.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience managing a broad range of solid tumors and complex oncology cases.
  • Familiarity with AI scribe technology, EHR documentation templates, medical chart review, or clinical annotation projects.

Application Process

  • Candidates will complete an AI interview to assess their relevant clinical expertise and experience.
