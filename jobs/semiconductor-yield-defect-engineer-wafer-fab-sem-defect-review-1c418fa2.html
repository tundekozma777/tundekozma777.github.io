Semiconductor Yield / Defect Engineer (Wafer Fab, SEM Defect Review)

Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Conduct SEM (Scanning Electron Microscope) defect review and accurate classification of defects observed in wafer fabrication processes.
  • Interpret and analyze yield loss mechanisms, correlating defect patterns with process-related Issues.
  • Evaluate excursion events and make informed lot-hold or release recommendations based on empirical evidence and industry best practices.
  • Review, interpret, and annotate wafer sort and test floor date, including bin maps and parametric datasets.
  • Identify defect paretos and contribute to source attribution analysis to trace root causes.
  • Document observations, communicate technical findings, and provide actionable feedback in clear, concise written and verbal formats for integration Into AI learning modules.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive hands-on experience in wafer fab environments, with a focus on SEM-based defect review and yield analysis,.
  • Demonstrated expertise in production fabs with integrated sort/test floors; experience limited to research cleanrooms does not qualify.
  • Proficiency with defect classification methodologies, excursion management, and yield improvement strategies.
  • Strong ability to analyze wafer sort/bin data and interpret test floor metrics to Inform defect and yield investigations.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, able to explain complex technical concepts to diverse audiences.
  • Background in process integration or defect/yield engineering at major semiconductor manufacturing facilities.
  • Experience as a retired or former fab engineer considered a valuable asset.
