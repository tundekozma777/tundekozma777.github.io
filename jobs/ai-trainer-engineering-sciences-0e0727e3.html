  • Relevant specializations may include mechanical and aerospace engineering, electrical and computer engineering, electronics and embedded systems, chemical and process engineering, civil and structural engineering, manufacturing and industrial engineering, robotics and controls, energy systems, and biomedical engineering.
  • Designs taken into production Simulations validated against test data Complex engineering failures personally diagnosed Patents, publications, or technical reports Public engineering code, scripts, models, or simulations Open-source hardware or engineering-software contributions Engineering competition results Reproducible notebooks, benchmarks, or technical projects Confidential employer or customer materials should not be shared.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience creating AI-training tasks, reference solutions, rubrics, graders, or benchmark problems.
  • Experience reviewing other AI trainers’ technical submissions.
  • A master’s degree, PhD, or substantial professional experience in a relevant engineering discipline.
  • Experience with command-line tools, scripting interfaces, configuration files, solver APIs, or programmatic engineering software.
  • Experience validating computational results against calculations, simulations, measurements, or physical tests.
  • Prior experience with long-horizon or agentic AI evaluations.
  • Availability to begin immediately.

Engagement Details

Application Process
