Engineering & Design

Mechanical Design Engineer – CAD & Manufacturing

Remote$40–$80 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Create and interpret professional engineering drawings, ensuring all relevant details are conveyed for manufacturing.
  • Translate complex design requirements into clear, manufacturable CAD specifications, with a focus on accuracy and precision.
  • Produce detailed 2D and 3D models using industry-standard tools (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Onshape, Inventor, Creo, NX) and reference models in FreeCAD.
  • Apply advanced principles of tolerancing, dimensioning, and Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T) to all deliverables.
  • Document and communicate manufacturing processes and design intent in clear, well-structured written and verbal formats.
  • Review, critique, and improve existing engineering documentation to enhance clarity and manufacturability.
  • Contribute insights on design for manufacture (DFM) and help optimize parts for real-world production scenarios.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive industry experience in mechanical design, with parts successfully transitioned from concept through to manufacturing.
  • Expert-level proficiency in CAD software, with demonstrated capability across multiple platforms (e.g., SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Onshape, Inventor, Creo, NX).
  • Solid hands-on knowledge of FreeCAD for interpreting or referencing models.
  • Strong command of drafting standards, GD&T, folerancing, and the creation of technical documentation suitable for manufacturing.
  • Deep understanding of various manufacturing processes, material selection, and DFM best practices.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, able to document decisions and explain engineering concepts clearly.
  • Experience collaborating on engineering projects remotely and sharing feedback in a constructive, professional manner.
