Senior Metallurgist / Welding Inspector

Global, fully remote$40–$60 per hourContractor (~10 hours per week)

Responsibilities

  • Evaluate and interpret metallographic microstructures to determine material acceptability and conformance to standards.
  • Conduct heat treatment process analysis and acceptance reviews in accordance with AMS 2750, ASTM E112, and related US specifications.
  • Perform coating inspection tasks aligned with NACE/AMPP and SSPC protocols, ensuring surface integrity and protection requirements are met.
  • Review and disposition additive manufacturing (LPBF) defects based on detailed build and cross-sectional data.
  • Reference and apply NADCAP, AWS, ASTM, and AMS standards toa variety of metal samples, weldments, coatings, and AM builds.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Atleast 8 years of hands-on experience making high-stakes accept/reject calls on micrographs, weld macros, fracture surfaces, coatings, or LPBF additive builds.
  • Advanced knowledge of US and international standards including ASTM, AMS, AWS, NADCAP, NACE/AMPP, and SSPC.
  • Current or prior AWS Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) credentials, or equivalent metallurgical certification.
  • Familiarity with additive manufacturing processes, especially laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) and associated defect mechanisms.
