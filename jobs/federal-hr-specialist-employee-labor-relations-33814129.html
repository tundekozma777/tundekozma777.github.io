Federal HR Specialist (Employee & Labor Relations)

$60–$90 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Develop and articulote realistic federal HR scenarios and case studies grounded in Employee & Labor Relations, drawing from practical experience.
  • Craft detailed reference answers and rationales based on federal HR policy and best practices.
  • Review, assess, and provide constructive written feedback on material submitted by fellow subject-matter experts.
  • Ensure project deliverables accurately reflect the nuances of federal HR decision-making, especially in Employee & Labor Relations contexts.
  • Collaborate with project coordinators to resolve ambiguities or identify areas needing additional depth or clarity.
  • Maintain clarity and precision by documenting the reasoning behind recommendations and scenario responses.
  • Adhere to defined project guidelines, milestone requirements, and quality standards for all deliverables.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Current or former federal HR experience at the GS-0201 or GS-0260 series, or equivalent level in government service,.
  • Extensive, practical knowledge of Title 5 HR matters, including employee relations, labor relations, classification, or workforce restructuring,.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to convey complex concepts clearly and concisely.
  • Direct hands-on experience managing or advising on adverse actions, grievances, or appeals within a federal context.
  • Background in position classification, rating and ranking, or experience with reduction in force processes.
  • Meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to accuracy in scenario development and feedback.
  • Based in the United States, familiar with current federal HR policies and procedures.
