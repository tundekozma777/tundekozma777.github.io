Responsibilities

  • Create original, high-difficulty question-and-answer pairs at the cutting edge of your discipline to rigorously assess AI model capabilities.
  • Source, verify, and thoroughly document answers using primary sources and authoritative references, including clear citations and reasoning.

  • Masters or PhD completed, active candidacy, or equivalent research experience as a specialist, researcher, assistant professor or professor in your domain.
  • Demonstrated track record of scholarly research, in-depth subject matter expertise, and familiarity with primary literature.
  • Exceptional analytical thinking, close attention to detail, and strong written communication skills in English.
  • Experience sourcing and triangulating information from authoritative references.
  • Proven self-direction and reliability in delivering expert-level work independently within remote engagements.
  • Skillin composing challenging, original, and methodologically sound questions.
  • Prior experience in AI training, data labeling, or model evaluation is required.

Engagement Details

  • Compensation is output-based; experts are paid per task that meets the project specifications. The time required to complete work may vary depending on the expert's experience and workflow. Minimum submission requirements apply. Experts must submit a minimum of tasks per week.
