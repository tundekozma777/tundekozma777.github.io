Remote$175–$300 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Demonstrated ability to both produce and review analytical and presentation materials, ensuring they meet senior banker or client standards.
  • Mastery in Excel modeling, including troubleshooting, scenario analysis, and presentation of outputs with clear rationale.
  • Direct experience conducting company/industry research, target screening, comps analysis, and pitchbook preparation.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication abilities, with a focus on clarity, accuracy, and actionable feedback.
  • Ability to identify and clearly articulate quantitative inaccuracies and strategic weaknesses in transaction materials.
