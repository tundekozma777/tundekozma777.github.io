SHIP Medicare Counselor

Remote$30–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review sample Medicare questions and flag any that don’t reflect what real beneficiaries ask.
  • Write rubrics defining a correct, complete answer for enrollment scenarios, including initial enrollment, special enrollment periods, and Part B late penalties.
  • Cover Medigap vs. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan comparison in your rubrics, including key differences and when each option fits.
  • Evaluate AI-generated answers against your rubrics, flagging errors, omissions, and outdated guidance.
  • Give clear written feedback to the project team, working asynchronously.

Required Qualifications

  • Active or recent SHIP certification, or former SSA Medicare enrollment experience.
  • 1+ years advising on initial enrollment, special enrollment periods, Part B late penalties, Medigap vs. Medicare Advantage, and Part D.
  • Based in the US.
  • Able to start quickly and commit 20-30 hours/week.
  • Clear, precise writing.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience creating guides, training materials, or rubrics for Medicare counseling.
  • Experience counseling a diverse range of beneficiaries, including rural and non-English-speaking clients.
