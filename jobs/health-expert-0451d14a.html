Health Expert

Remote$20–$45 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review and analyze health-related content, including clinical scenarios, medication information, and patient care records.
  • Annotate and provide structured feedback on long-form and multi-turn conversational data in health contexts.
  • Craft comprehensive written explanations and responses to complex medical, nursing, or pharmaceutical queries.
  • Ensure accuracy, clarity, and compliance in all provided materials, using up-to-date health science standards.
  • Collaborate with project managers and other experts to refine guidelines and improve data quality.
  • Apply careful reading skills to compare, contrast, and evaluate paired responses for depth and correctness.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in Nursing, Medicine, Pharmacy, Health Science, or a related field.
  • Experience as a doctor, medical student, nurse, pharmacy technician, or health science specialist.
  • Native or near-native proficiency in written English, with strong verbal communication abilities.
  • Prior experience in data annotation or similar project-based health data work is a strong plus.
  • Exceptional attention to detail in reading, comparing, and evaluating nuanced written content.
  • Demonstrated ability to communicate complex health information accurately and succinctly.
