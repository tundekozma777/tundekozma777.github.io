Brand and Social Creative Expert

Remote, worldwideContractor

About the Role

Review marketing and social media creative for a worldwide remote project. Compare posts, ads, videos, and campaign ideas against their brief, audience, and platform.

Responsibilities

  • Assess which creative best meets a campaign goal for its intended audience.
  • Check alignment with the brief, brand voice, brand rules, and platform conventions.
  • Flag potential issues involving claims, disclosures, rights, or platform policies.
  • Explain each decision in concise written feedback.

Required Qualifications

  • Professional experience creating or directing brand, marketing, or social media work for a company, agency, or client.
  • Experience with campaign briefs, approvals, publishing, and results.
  • Familiarity with at least two major social platforms or paid social channels.
  • Ability to explain creative decisions clearly in written English.

Application Process

Apply with a résumé and short profile. The hiring team reviews your experience against the project needs. Selected applicants receive guidelines and complete an initial task for review.
