  • Advanced research skills with a track record of verifying complex claims against authoritative sources.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills to articulate findings and feedback.
  • Demonstrated ability to precisely follow detailed instructions and evaluation frameworks.
  • Background in adversarial or “stomp the model” task design is advantageous.
  • Graduate-level research, experience at a Fortune 100 company, or professional fluency in a non-English language a plus.

Engagement Details
