About the Role

Job Title: Chief of Staff

You'll operate at the intersection of strategy, operations, and execution, helping the CRO focus on what matters most, accelerate decision-making, and turn ambitious goals into measurable outcomes.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in ambiguity, thinks strategically, moves fast, and takes end-to-end ownership of complex challenges in a fast-paced AI environment.

Responsibilities

  • Optimize the CRO's time and priorities through calendar management, executive briefings, meeting preparation, and follow-ups.
  • Drive cross-functional initiatives, align stakeholders, track milestones, and ensure critical deliverables are completed.
  • Identify inefficiencies, improve processes, and build scalable systems that increase organizational effectiveness.
  • Develop reporting frameworks, monitor KPls, and surface actionable insights to support business decisions.
  • Prepare presentations, draft internal communications, and ensure alignment on executive priorities.
  • Research business challenges, evaluate opportunities, and develop data-driven recommendations.
  • Own high-impact, ambiguous initiatives that fall outside traditional departmental responsibilities.
  • Leverage AI tools and automation to streamline workflows, improve productivity, and accelerate execution.

Required Qualifications

  • 4+ years of experience in strategy, business operations, management consulting, revenue operations, or similar roles.
  • Strong business acumen, analytical skills, and the ability to turn ambiguous problems into actionable plans.
  • Proven ability to manage complex initiatives, prioritize effectively, and deliver results with minimal oversight.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders and communicate with senior leadership.
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools and automation to improve workflows and operational efficiency.
  • A proactive, resourceful, and ownership-driven mindset with strong judgment and attention to detail.
  • Ability to thrive in fast-moving environments, manage competing priorities, and navigate ambiguity.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting a CRO, CEO, or other C-level executive.
  • Familiarity with go-to-market strategies, revenue operations, sales pipelines, and CRM systems.
  • Experience building KPI dashboards, executive reporting systems, or operational workflows from scratch.
  • Background in management consulting, strategic finance, or a founder's office.
  • Experience with LLMs, AI agents, and workflow automation platforms.
