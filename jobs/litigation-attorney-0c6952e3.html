Litigation Attorney

Remote$80–$170 per hour (listed range, task-based pay)Contractor

Responsibilities

  • Analyze legal disputes and develop comprehensive litigation strategies based on case specifics and applicable law.
  • Conduct in-depth legal research on statutes, regulations, and case law to inform project deliverables.
  • Draft and review a wide range of legal documents, including pleadings, motions, and briefs, for accuracy and relevance.
  • Assess evidence, contracts, and discovery materials to provide critical analysis and insights.
  • Provide clear written and verbal explanations of litigation principles, processes, and best practices for project use.
  • Collaborate with other legal experts to ensure solutions reflect current litigation standards and practices.
  • Deliver detailed case management recommendations and outline procedural strategies for hypothetical or real-life scenarios.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree from an accredited law school.
  • Active law license in at least one U.S. jurisdiction.
  • Extensive experience in civil or commercial litigation, including case strategy, discovery, and motion practice.
  • Demonstrated excellence in legal research, writing, and analysis.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with a focus on clarity and precision.
  • Strong organizational abilities and attention to detail in managing multiple case assignments or deliverables.
  • Experience providing consultation or training on litigation topics is a plus.

Engagement Details

  • Compensation is output-based; experts are paid per task that meets the project specifications. The time required to complete work may vary depending on the expert's experience and workflow. Minimum submission requirements apply. Experts must submit a minimum of tasks per week.
