British English Voice Expert

Remote$40–$65 per hourContract

About the Role

We are looking for native British English speakers to support an AI training project focused on evaluating English audio recordings. In this role, you will use your knowledge of British English pronunciation, accents, natural speech, and audio quality to assess recordings and provide accurate feedback.

Responsibilities

  • Listen to and evaluate short British English audio recordings.
  • Assess whether speech sounds natural and native to British English.
  • Evaluate pronunciation, accent, fluency, tone, and natural speech patterns.
  • Identify unnatural, inconsistent, or incorrect elements in spoken English.
  • Provide clear and structured feedback on audio recordings.
  • Follow project guidelines and maintain accuracy and consistency.

Required Qualifications

  • Native-level British English proficiency.
  • Strong understanding of British English pronunciation, accents, and natural speech patterns.
  • B2-level English proficiency or above.
  • Excellent listening and analytical skills.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Ability to provide clear written feedback.
  • Comfortable working independently in a remote environment.
  • Ability to successfully complete the required screening test.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience as a voice actor, voice coach, audio engineer, or similar professional.
  • Background in voice-over, speech analysis, audio engineering, or sound production.
  • Experience in linguistics, phonetics, language teaching, media, or communications.
  • Previous experience evaluating speech or audio recordings is a plus.
