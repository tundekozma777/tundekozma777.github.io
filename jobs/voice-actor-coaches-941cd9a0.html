Languages & AI Quality

Voice Actor/Coaches

Remote$30–$70 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Analyze short speech recordings and produce precise, structured written assessments using professional voice terminology.
  • Identify and describe vocal characteristics such as age, gender, accent or dialect (when confident), vocal quality, resonance, phonation type, pitch, intonation, breath support, rhythm, and tempo.
  • Evaluate emotional tone, energy, articulation clarity, and overall delivery style, noting nuances between scripted, spontaneous, or conversational samples.
  • Assess audio production aspects, including room sound, device type (microphone, headset, phone), and recording environment quality (studio or home).
  • Deliver observations in clear, detailed written reports, using consistent technical language to facilitate AI model training and evaluation.
  • Collaborate asynchronously via digital platforms, providing objective, high-quality input to enhance voice and speech systems.
  • Maintain confidentiality and accuracy throughout all analyses and documentation.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Professional background in voice acting, voice coaching, dialect coaching, vocal pedagogy, or similar fields.
  • Strong understanding of speech analysis, prosody, intonation, phonetics, accent and dialect variation, and vocal quality assessment.
  • Experience with audio or sound engineering, especially in evaluating recording quality and acoustic environments.
  • Familiarity with technical vocal concepts such as resonance, placement, and breath control.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with meticulous attention to detail.
  • Fluency in English (B2 or higher); native proficiency in any language is welcome.
  • Reliable internet connection and high-quality headphones to ensure accurate audio assessment.
