Softwood Lumber Grader / Sawmill Quality Supervisor (Visual Grading)

Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review and assess descriptions and images of sawn softwood lumber for accurate visual grading according to published rules (NLGA, WWPA, SPIB, WCLIB).
  • Identify and measure lumber defects including knots, wane, checks, shake, and warp, and determine corresponding grade limits.
  • Make clear grade calls on sample pieces based on rulebook criteria, documenting the decision-making process in writing.
  • Evaluate grade stamping accuracy and provide feedback on proper application and compliance.
  • Analyze grade recovery and quality control scenarios, offering insight into optimization possibilities.
  • Reference the specific rule(s) that justify each grade decision, ensuring technical rigor.
  • Deliver written and verbal explanations for decisions to support AI model training and development.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Active or recent certification as a softwood lumber grader or sawmill quality control supervisor from a recognized agency (e.g., NLGA, WWPA, SPIB, WCLIB).
  • Demonstrated expertise in visual lumber grading and defect identification across common softwood species.
  • Proven ability to interpret and apply industry grading rules with confidence and accuracy.
  • Experience providing written and verbal feedback or training on quality standards.
  • Familiarity with grade stamping procedures, grade recovery metrics, and quality control best practices.
  • Strong attention to detail, technical documentation skills, and ability to articulate decisions clearly.
  • Recent practical experience in a sawmill environment is highly valued.
