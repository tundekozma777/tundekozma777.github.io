Member of Technical Staff, Cortex Lab (applied AI research)

About the Role

Evaluate and improve LLM-powered agents that operate in enterprise workflows. The role combines applied research, failure analysis, and Python engineering.

Responsibilities

  • Develop benchmarks, evaluation frameworks, and datasets for AI agents and complex workflows.
  • Find and prioritize failures in production, then run experiments to test fixes and measure reliability.
  • Create training and evaluation data, task specifications, and feedback mechanisms.
  • Build Python evaluation pipelines, experimental tools, and research infrastructure.
  • Work with researchers, domain experts, and customers to deploy and improve systems, set evaluation priorities, and report findings.

Required Qualifications

  • Background in applied AI research, research engineering, ML, or LLM systems.
  • Experience evaluating agents on tool use, multi-step workflows, and task completion.
  • Strong Python skills and experience with evaluation pipelines, experimental tooling, or ML infrastructure.
  • Experience designing benchmarks, datasets, metrics, and experiments.
  • Ability to diagnose agent failures and turn ambiguous problems into experiments and measurable improvements.
  • Understanding of LLM behavior, agent architectures, reliability, robustness, and generalization, plus strong cross-team communication.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with enterprise or production agents, reinforcement learning, reward modeling, or post-training.
  • Experience developing research infrastructure, evaluation tools, or automated experiments.
  • Frontier AI lab or applied research experience, publications, or contributions to open benchmarks and frameworks.
  • Direct collaboration with enterprise customers or technical stakeholders.

Compensation and Benefits

The posting header advertises $300,000–$700,000 per year in compensation. The detailed notice lists $200,000–$250,000 annual base salary (USD). Employees are eligible for equity and may receive performance-based bonuses, depending on the role and company policies.

Listed benefits include health-insurance premium reimbursement of up to 100%, paid time off, and a 401(k) with employer matching.
