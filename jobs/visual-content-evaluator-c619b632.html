Visual Content Evaluator

Remote$25–$35 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Upload original images you own that strictly follow provided project specifications.
  • Compare each original image with its AI-generated counterpart, performing meticulous, side-by-side assessments.
  • Identify and document all visual discrepancies, including subtle flaws in lighting, perspective, texture, anatomy, and color fidelity.
  • Detect and flag common AI image artifacts such as warped hands, smudged surfaces, inconsistent shadows, broken patterns, or misshapen text.
  • Compose clear, accurate, and concise written descriptions for each identified issue, pinpointing exact locations within the images.
  • Consistently apply a high standard of quality assurance by following detailed project guidelines and prioritizing accuracy over speed.
  • Maintain thorough, constructive feedback throughout your evaluations, ensuring actionable insights for model improvement.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Based in the United States (non-negotiable project requirement).
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills in English, producing error-free feedback and detailed image notes.
  • Keen visual acuity with a proven ability to detect even subtle differences between similar images.
  • Background in photography, digital imaging, graphic design, photo editing, or a closely related discipline, with the precise vocabulary to describe technical visual issues.
  • Understanding of AI image generation processes and typical failure points.
  • Demonstrated experience in image evaluation, data annotation, comparison, or similar visual quality assurance contexts.
  • Reliable, detail-oriented approach with a commitment to careful, comprehensive review on every assignment.
