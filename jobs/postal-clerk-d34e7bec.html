Postal Clerk

Remote, United States$30–$45 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review sample postal questions and flag any that don’t sound like what real customers ask.
  • Write rubrics that define a correct, complete answer on mail classes, shipping options, postage, fees, and shipping labels.
  • Cover package tracking, missing or damaged mail, change of address and mail forwarding, and international mailing and customs forms in your rubrics.
  • Evaluate AI-generated answers against your rubrics, flagging errors, omissions, and outdated guidance.
  • Share clear written feedback with the project team, working asynchronously.

Required Qualifications

  • Current, former, or retired USPS frontline experience (postal clerk, sales and service associate, or retail associate).
  • 1+ years helping customers in person at a post office counter.
  • Working knowledge of mail classes, shipping options, postage, tracking, and change of address.
  • US-based.
  • Able to start quickly and commit 15-25 hours/week.
  • Clear, precise writing.
  • Not a fit: Mail carrier, mail processing, or private shipping store experience alone.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with international mail and customs documentation.
  • Experience handling missing or damaged mail claims.
  • Experience creating guides, FAQs, or training materials.
  • Experience helping a diverse range of customers, including rural and non-English-speaking communities.
