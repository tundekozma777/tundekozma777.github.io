About the Role

Job Title: Strategic Project Lead, Robotics

As a Strategic Projects Lead (Robotics), you will own robotics data pipelines end-to-end. You'll manage everything from data capture to final delivery, ensuring high quality, scalability, and efficiency across the entire pipeline. You'll work closely with engineering, operations, and finance, and play a key role in driving execution on complex, high-impact projects.

Responsibilities

  • Own robotics data pipelines end-to-end with clear accountability for quality and delivery.
  • Design and optimize large-scale data collection and processing pipelines.
  • Scale operations by balancing cost, quality, and throughput.
  • Deploy and manage hardware systems (multi-camera rigs, RGB/stereo/IMU sensors).
  • Lead data transformation pipelines aligned with strict client schemas and formats.
  • Identify bottlenecks and build automation for validation, QA, and reporting.
  • Define and enforce capture protocols, workflows, and operational standards.
  • Translate client requirements into executable systems and processes.
  • Work closely with engineering, operations, and finance to drive execution.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong experience with robotics or high-throughput data pipelines.
  • Hands-on with sensor systems, FPS constraints, and multi-camera setups.
  • Analytical and systems-oriented; able to optimize cost vs speed vs quality.
  • Clear communicator across technical and non-technical teams.
  • Experience with large-scale data processing, storage, and delivery systems.
  • Proven ability to define schemas, protocols, and operational standards.
  • Track record of driving cross-functional execution and improving systems.
  • High ownership, fast execution, and comfort in ambiguous environments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience scaling robotics data operations from pilot to production.
  • Built internal tools for data validation, QA, or pipeline automation.
  • Experience working directly with clients on technical data requirements.
