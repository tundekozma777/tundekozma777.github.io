Medicare Enrollment Specialist

Remote, United States$40–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review sample questions about signing up for Medicare and flag any that don't sound like what real people ask.
  • Write rubrics that define a correct, complete answer on initial enrollment, special enrollment periods, and Part B late penalties, including how enrollment ties to Social Security benefits.
  • Cover Medigap vs. Medicare Advantage and Part D in your rubrics, with a focus on scenarios involving employer or retiree coverage.
  • Cover how employer coverage interacts with Medicare, including employer size, primary vs. secondary coverage, COBRA, and creditable coverage.
  • Evaluate AI-generated answers against your rubrics, flagging errors, omissions, and common misconceptions.
  • Share clear written feedback with the project team, working asynchronously.

Required Qualifications

  • Former SSA Medicare enrollment experience, or employer benefits/HR experience advising employees on Medicare.
  • 1+ years advising on initial enrollment, special enrollment periods, Part B late penalties, Medigap vs. Medicare Advantage, and Part D.
  • Working knowledge of Social Security and employer coverage coordination, including creditable coverage.
  • US-based.
  • Able to start quickly and commit 20-30 hours/week.
  • Clear, precise writing.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with premium adjustments (such as IRMAA), working while enrolled, or HSA rules alongside Medicare.
  • Experience creating guides, counseling scripts, or training materials.
  • Experience helping a diverse range of beneficiaries, including rural and non-English-speaking clients.
