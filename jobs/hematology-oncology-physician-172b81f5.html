Hematology/Oncology Physician

United States, Remote$100–$180 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review and annotate AI Generated transcripts of outpatient hematology/oncology clinic conversations for accuracy, clinical completeness, and clarity.
  • Assess AI Generated documentation sections such as History of Present Illness (HPI), interval histories, and Assessment & Plans (A&Ps), providing input on appropriate information inclusion.
  • Identify and comment on nuances of chemotherapy cycles, treatment toxicities, laboratory discussions, and decision-making processes in clinical documentation.
  • Provide feedback on how well AI Generated transcripts reflect real-world clinical reasoning and patient interactions, especially during follow-up visits.
  • Contribute to the evaluation and improvement of AI scribe outputs or EHR note templates, as needed.

Preferred Qualifications

  • MD, DO, or equivalent with board certification or eligibility in Hematology and/or Oncology.
  • At least 2 years of recent outpatient clinical experience post-fellowship, focusing on patient follow-up visits, chemotherapy management, and longitudinal care.
  • Demonstrated proficiency in independently writing HPIs, interval histories, and A&Ps for outpatient oncology or hematology care.
  • Comprehensive understanding of chemotherapy treatment cycles, side effect management, and lab result interpretation in an outpatient setting.
  • Experience with ambient AI scribe solutions, EHR note templates, annotation projects, or medical chart review (preferred but not required).
  • Familiarity with best practices in outpatient oncology workflow and a track record of clear, patient-centered clinical interactions.
