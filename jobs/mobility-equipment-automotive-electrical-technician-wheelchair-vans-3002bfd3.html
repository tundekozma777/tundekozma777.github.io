Mobility Equipment / Automotive Electrical Technician (Wheelchair Vans)

Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Analyze and interpret complex automotive 12V electrical wiring diagrams for wheelchair-accessible van conversions.
  • Diagnose and describe solutions to electrical faults in mobility systems, including lifts, ramps, and controllers.
  • Document service and repair procedures for kneeling systems, door automation, and hand control integrations.
  • Explain troubleshooting sequences and decision logic when addressing electrical and mobility system issues.
  • Review and provide feedback on technician training materials, improving clarity and instructional quality.
  • Contribute in-depth, scenario-based written and verbal responses to simulated service cases, ensuring detailed and accurate information.
  • Identify common challenges in mobility van conversions and recommend best practices for diagnostics and repairs.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive background as a mobility technician within wheelchair van conversion or dealership environments.
  • Proficiency in reading and interpreting automotive electrical schematics and conducting 12V diagnostics.
  • Hands-on experience with mobility conversion equipment such as lifts, ramps, kneeling systems, and hand controls.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills; ability to convey complex technical concepts with clarity.
  • Ability to break down and document logical diagnostic sequences for training or instructional purposes.
  • Experience with service training or technician coaching is highly valued.
  • ASE A6 Electrical/Electronic Systems certification or NMEDA technician training is a plus.
