Senior MBSE Engineer - SysML v2

Remote$55–$120 per hourContractor

About the Role

In this role, you’ll apply your systems engineering knowledge to develop reference models, assess AI-generated SysML v2 outputs, and establish clear standards for technical accuracy and model quality.

Your expertise will help evaluate how effectively AI systems interpret engineering requirements, represent complex system architectures, and produce consistent, traceable models.

Responsibilities

  • Interpret and translate engineering requirements into accurate SysML v2 textual models, emphasizing model fidelity and traceability.
  • Develop reference solutions and exemplar architectures in SysML v2 to serve as benchmarks for AI evaluation tasks.
  • Create, refine, and document evaluation rubrics to assess the quality and correctness of SysML v2 models generated by AI systems.
  • Review, assess, and provide structured feedback on SysML v2 models, ensuring technical accuracy and adherence to modeling best practices.
  • Analyze requirements, Concepts of Operations (ConOps), Interface Control Documents (ICDs), and architectural specifications to inform modeling efforts.
  • Collaborate asynchronously through written and verbal communication to clarify project deliverables and resolve technical ambiguities.

Required Qualifications

  • Professional experience in systems engineering, with hands-on expertise in SysML v1 and some exposure to SysML v2, or demonstrated experience with SysML v2 textual modeling.
  • Ability to interpret engineering requirements and translate them into technically accurate system models.
  • Understanding of model-based systems engineering principles, requirements traceability, and system architecture.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Strong understanding of requirements engineering, system architecture, interface control, and model traceability.
  • Experience working with ConOps, ICDs, and other foundational systems engineering documentation.
  • Strong technical writing and communication skills, particularly in remote or asynchronous environments.
  • Experience in complex engineering domains such as aerospace, space, defense, automotive, maritime, or communications.
  • Familiarity with model verification, technical review, evaluation rubric development, or model quality assessment.
  • Exposure to AI, machine learning, or data-driven engineering projects.

Engagement Details

  • Independent contractor engagement. Task-based work with variable hours, estimated at about 15 hours per week. The initial pilot is expected to run for approximately four weeks to three months.

Application Process

  • Candidates will complete an AI interview as part of the application process.
