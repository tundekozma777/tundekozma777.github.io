Personal Financial Advisor

Northern America, Latin America$90–$110 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review and provide feedback on personal financial planning scenarios based on your experience advising individual clients.
  • Evaluate investment strategies, retirement plans, and financial recommendations tailored to individual client needs.
  • Analyze financial situations involving savings, investments, debt management, tax planning, and long-term financial goals.
  • Share insights from your experience working one-on-one with clients, including assessing financial needs, risk tolerance, and investment preferences.
  • Review financial planning materials for accuracy, regulatory compliance, and industry best practices.
  • Explain financial concepts, planning strategies, and recommendations clearly in written and verbal formats.
  • Collaborate with another financial expert to discuss different approaches, compare recommendations, and contribute to AI training.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Professional experience working directly with individual clients or families in a one-on-one financial advisory role.
  • Experience as a Personal Financial Advisor, Financial Planner, Wealth Manager, Investment Advisor, or similar client-facing professional.
  • Strong knowledge of personal financial planning, including retirement, investments, tax planning, estate planning, and wealth management.
  • Hands-on experience creating personalized financial plans and investment recommendations based on individual client goals.
  • Experience assessing clients’ financial situations, risk tolerance, investment objectives, and long-term financial needs.
  • Good understanding of financial products, portfolio management, asset allocation, and relevant regulatory requirements.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to explain financial concepts clearly.
  • Comfortable collaborating with another financial expert and available to work during overlapping North American or Latin American time zones.
