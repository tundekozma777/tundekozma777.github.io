About the Role

Turn reinforcement learning research into production systems by building training environments, pipelines, and evaluations.

Responsibilities

  • Build self-contained environments for complex tasks, including rewards, verifiers, and evaluation logic.
  • Scale episode pipelines and training processes for reproducible experiments.
  • Automate synthetic data generation, grading, validation, and feedback.
  • Fine-tune open-source RL models using internal datasets and custom training strategies.
  • Benchmark model capabilities, robustness, and data quality and contribute to releases and analysis.

Required Qualifications

  • Deep reinforcement learning experience covering environment design and training dynamics.
  • Experience scaling RL systems, training pipelines, or experimentation frameworks.
  • Experience automating synthetic data pipelines, evaluating models, and fine-tuning open-source ML models.
  • Clear technical writing and ability to collaborate in a fast-moving research team.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Published benchmarks, evaluations, or research artifacts.
  • Familiarity with evaluation platforms and infrastructure for large-scale RL experiments.

Compensation and Benefits

The posting header advertises $400,000–$800,000 per year in compensation. The detailed notice lists $200,000–$300,000 annual base salary (USD). Employees are eligible for equity and may receive performance-based bonuses, depending on the role and company policies.

Listed benefits include health-insurance premium reimbursement of up to 100%, paid time off, and a 401(k) with employer matching.
