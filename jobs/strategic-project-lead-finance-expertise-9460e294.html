About the Role

Job Title: Strategic Project Lead, Finance Expertise

As a Strategic Projects Lead (SPL) — Finance, you will own end-to-end execution for projects with our top AI Lab customers, with a strong focus on Finance. You will translate customer needs into clear project specifications, design and launch finance-focused data pipelines, and align Operations and Finance domain experts to deliver quickly and at a high quality.

Responsibilities

  • Own projects end to end, from pipeline framing and scoping through execution and delivery.
  • Translate customer needs into clear, actionable project specifications for Finance workflows.
  • Design and continuously improve finance-focused pipelines for usability, accuracy, quality, and scalability.
  • Align Operations and Finance domain experts for rapid deployment.
  • Set delivery milestones and drive a tight execution cadence.
  • Own client communication, keeping it clear, direct, and consistent.
  • Manage Finance domain experts and project leads to ensure quality and delivery.
  • Provide daily internal updates with progress, blockers, and explicit next steps.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong background in Finance, Financial Engineering, Economics, Accounting, or a related field.
  • Exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills, particularly when working with senior Finance experts.
  • Strong ownership, urgency, and attention to detail.
  • Startup and entrepreneurial mindset with a bias toward execution.
  • 2+ years of experience leading complex projects, operational workflows, or finance-related initiatives.
  • Ability to turn ambiguous customer requirements into clear plans and execute rapidly.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-intensity environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working with AI/ML companies, AI Labs, or data annotation/evaluation projects.
  • Experience managing finance-focused, technical, or data-intensive projects.
  • Familiarity with financial modeling, investment analysis, financial markets, accounting, or other complex Finance workflows.
  • Familiarity with data pipelines, AI training workflows, and human-in-the-loop operations.
