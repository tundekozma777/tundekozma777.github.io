Freight Railcar Carman / Car Foreman (AAR Repair)

Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review real-world inspection findings and assess the accuracy and completeness of reported repairs on freight railcars.
  • Apply AAR Field Manual and Office Manual rules to validate repair decisions and documentation.
  • Evaluate compliance with FRA Part 215 repair standards and ensure thorough repair documentation.
  • Analyze freight car component issues, such as wheels, brakes, and structural elements, to determine appropriate repair actions and billing job codes.
  • Provide detailed written and verbal feedback explaining applicable AAR rules, billing codes, and the rationale behind repair decisions.
  • Participate in the assessment of mechanical department documentation for accuracy, completeness, and regulatory compliance.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive experience as a journeyman carman, car foreman, car inspector, or mechanical department draftsman in Class I or short-line railroad settings or contract repair shops.
  • Expert-level knowledge of AAR Field Manual and Office Manual, including practical application to real-world repairs and billing scenarios.
  • Thorough understanding of FRA Part 215 and current industry repair documentation practices.
  • Proven ability to inspect, diagnose, and specify repairs for all major freight car components.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with attention to detail in technical explanations and feedback.
  • Familiarity with inspection record-keeping, repair billing codes, and AAR compliance requirements.
  • Background in training, mentoring, or knowledge sharing within mechanical or technical railcar roles (nice to have).
