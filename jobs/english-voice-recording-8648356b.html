micro] is engaging native and near-native English speakers for a read-aloud speech recording project. Responsibilities

  • Read provided English prompts aloud, accurately and naturally, following the pronunciation and pacing guidelines.
  • Follow the instructions for each prompt exactly — including reading answer choices when the task asks for it — without adding your own responses or interpretations.
  • Record and submit clear audio files using the tools or platform provided.
  • Keep your delivery consistent across sessions: same clarity, same enunciation, same pace.
  • Re-record and resubmit when a take doesn’t meet the quality standard.
  • Flag prompts that are unclear, ambiguous, or have technical problems instead of guessing how to read them.
  • Stay in touch with project coordinators to align on quality expectations.
