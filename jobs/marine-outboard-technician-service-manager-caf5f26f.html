Marine Outboard Technician / Service Manager

Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Evaluate and provide detailed technical responses to scenarios involving outboard engine no-starts, overheating, and performance issues.
  • Analyze EFI fault codes, fuel and ignition system anomalies, and cooling system malfunctions in various outboard brands (Yamaha, Mercury, Suzuki, Honda, BRP).
  • Review rigging, propping, and repower recommendations for correctness and completeness based on real-world case studies.
  • Assess lower unit and gearcase issues, offering insights into common causes, repair techniques, and best practices.
  • Examine service documentation and warranty cases, identifying gaps or confirming adherence to OEM guidelines.
  • Deliver written and verbal explanations that clarify complex service and diagnostic processes for a technical audience.
  • Contribute to the creation of robust knowledge bases, training materials, and scenario assessments for AI learning.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive hands-on experience with outboard engine diagnostics, including EFI, ignition, fuel, and cooling systems.
  • Proficiency in rigging, repower procedures, and routine service protocols across multiple outboard brands.
  • Strong familiarity with OEM service procedures, warranty documentation, and technical guidance documents.
  • Demonstrated ability to assess and articulate correct service recommendations for diverse engine platforms.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with attention to technical accuracy and clarity.
  • Current or past role as an outboard technician or service manager within a dealer or OEM service network.
  • OEM Master Technician certification (Yamaha, Mercury, Suzuki, Honda, BRP) is a strong plus.
