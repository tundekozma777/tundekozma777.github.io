About the Role

Research how robots learn by testing vision-language-action models, generalist policies, and world models in simulation and on physical systems.

Responsibilities

  • Lead robotics experiments from teleoperation and data collection through simulation, policy deployment, and physical evaluation.
  • Build high-fidelity simulations from real-world data to validate robot policies.
  • Use a human-in-the-loop annotation platform to organize egocentric video, teleoperation demonstrations, and sensor data.
  • Analyze failures and sim-to-real gaps to improve datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation methods.
  • Prototype robot learning and policy optimization methods and take promising research through implementation.

Required Qualifications

  • Research background in robotics, embodied AI, robot learning, computer vision, or applied ML.
  • Hands-on experience building, training, or evaluating robot learning models or policies.
  • Python proficiency and experience with PyTorch, ROS, or robotics simulators.
  • Experience designing experiments with simulation, real-world data, policy evaluation, or hardware deployment.
  • Understanding of multimodal perception and sim-to-real transfer, with the ability to diagnose failures and work independently across teams.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with world models, reinforcement or imitation learning, or diffusion-based robot policies.
  • Hands-on work with teleoperation, robotics hardware, sensor integration, or physical policy deployment.
  • Familiarity with MuJoCo, Isaac Sim, or similar simulators and experience building multimodal robotics datasets.
  • Robotics or frontier AI lab experience, publications, open-source work, or benchmark contributions.

Compensation and Benefits
