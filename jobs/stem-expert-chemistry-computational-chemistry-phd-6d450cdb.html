STEM Expert – Chemistry & Computational Chemistry (PhD)

Remote (Worldwide)$45–$90 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Design, evaluate, and critique chemistry and computational chemistry problems, workflows, and reasoning chains to support AI benchmarking initiatives.
  • Provide expert-level insights on reaction mechanisms, synthesis planning, spectroscopy analysis, molecular simulations, and electronic structure methods.
  • Assess and optimize experimental protocols, simulation parameters, and software configurations for accuracy and reproducibility.
  • Analyze chemical, spectroscopic, and simulation data and provide actionable recommendations.
  • Leverage domain knowledge to guide the development and validation of chemistry-related AI models and tools.
  • Communicate complex scientific concepts clearly, both in writing and verbally, to technical and non-technical collaborators.
  • Document findings, methodologies, and best practices to ensure knowledge transfer across the customer’s team.
  • Areas of Expertise (we welcome applicants from any of the following):.
  • Organic synthesis & reaction mechanisms Inorganic & organometallic chemistry Physical & theoretical chemistry.
  • Analytical chemistry & spectroscopy Computational chemistry & molecular dynamics Cheminformatics & drug discovery Materials science & polymer chemistry.

Required Qualifications

  • PhD in chemistry, computational chemistry, chemical biology, materials science, or a closely related field.
  • Hands-on research lab experience, including wet lab experience for experimental subfields, or extensive simulation-heavy research for computational subfields.
  • Deep expertise in at least one of the areas listed above.
  • Professional written and spoken English (C1 or higher).
  • Proficiency in Python and/or industry-standard tools in your subfield (e.g., Gaussian, ORCA, Psi4, GROMACS, LAMMPS, RDKit).
  • Excellent quantitative, analytical, and scientific reasoning skills.
  • Outstanding written and verbal communication abilities, with the ability to clearly document and explain technical concepts.
  • Track record of rigorous scientific problem-solving in complex chemistry domains.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Academic experience (postdoc, research scientist, faculty, or equivalent), strongly preferred.
  • Peer-reviewed publications, patents, or contributions to open-source scientific software.
  • Experience with AI/ML-assisted chemistry workflows or benchmarking.
  • Background in drug discovery, reaction prediction, or retrosynthesis.
