Federal Budget Analyst

Remote, US only$60–$85 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Create realistic training examples of federal budget justification materials, including narratives, exhibits, and supporting analyses.
  • Write sample responses to OMB passback with clear, well-supported analysis, and annotate the reasoning behind each choice.
  • Document mid-year reprogramming scenarios, noting which actions require higher-level approval and how they stay consistent with spend plans.
  • Build worked examples that compare actual expenditures against planned obligations, evaluate fund availability, and explain variances.
  • Walk through year-end closeout step by step, including reconciliation with accounting records and resolution of discrepancies.
  • Write guidance on funding types, budget changes, and compliance requirements, drawing on how experienced analysts advise program managers.
  • Produce templates, process walkthroughs, and annotated examples that capture how federal budget work is actually done.
  • Review AI-generated budget content for accuracy, realism, and compliance, and explain what is wrong or missing so it can be corrected.

Preferred Qualifications

  • 5+ years of federal budget experience at about the GS-11/12 level or higher, in GS-O560, 0501, 0510, or comparable roles.
  • Experience with budget formulation, execution, and funds control.
  • Experience with OMB submissions, reprogramming, and year-end closeout.
  • Familiarity with federal accounting and budget execution systems.
  • Financial management certification (DoD FM, CGFM, or similar) a plus.
