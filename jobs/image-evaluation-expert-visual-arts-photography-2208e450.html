Image Evaluation Expert (Visual Arts / Photography)

Remote, United States$21–$35 per hour

About the Role

micro] is looking for AI Photo Raters to support a customer project focused on evaluating AI-generated images.

In this role, you'll use your visual judgment to compare original photographs with AI-generated versions, identify inconsistencies, and provide detailed written feedback. Your observations will help evaluate image quality and support the refinement of AI models.

We're looking for people with a strong eye for detail and an understanding of photography, visual design, or image editing. Prior experience working with AI is not required.

Responsibilities

  • Upload original photographs for use in structured image evaluation tasks.
  • Compare real photographs with AI-generated images to identify visual differences, inconsistencies, and artifacts.
  • Evaluate details such as lighting, perspective, anatomy, textures, and rendering quality.
  • Document your findings through clear, specific written feedback that explains what looks incorrect and why.
  • Apply your knowledge of photography and image editing to assess visual accuracy and image quality.
  • Handle submitted images and project materials with appropriate privacy and confidentiality.
  • Collaborate remotely with other contributors to address project goals and evaluation challenges.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong attention to visual detail and the ability to recognize subtle differences between images.
  • Clear written English communication skills, including the ability to explain visual observations accurately.
  • Critical thinking skills and the ability to provide constructive, evidence-based feedback.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience or expertise in digital photography, image editing, graphic design, or related visual arts.
  • Familiarity with image editing software such as Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom, or GIMP.
  • Experience with AI image generation tools, image comparison, or visual evaluation projects.
  • Ability to collaborate with others and communicate feedback constructively.

Engagement Details

  • This is a remote contractor opportunity for candidates based in the United States. The work involves submitting photographs, evaluating AI-generated images, and providing written feedback according to project requirements.
