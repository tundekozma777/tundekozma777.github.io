Operational Law Attorney (JAG) SME

Remote$95–$170 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Author and categorize boundary cases focusing on the application of the law of armed conflict (LOAC), including distinction, proportionality, and precautions in attack.
  • Render operational legal judgments on ambiguous scenarios, defending and documenting your reasoning in detail.
  • Validate and refine the legal framework underpinning the project's taxonomy to ensure its robustness under scrutiny.
  • Assess AI-generated responses for depth of reasoning, treatment of genuine legal uncertainty, and clarity of limitations.
  • Identify and flag both excessive caution as well as insufficient legal rigor in the model’s handling of complex military-legal questions.
  • Maintain comprehensive documentation of edge cases and the legal authorities supporting each conclusion.

Preferred Qualifications

  • JAG or equivalent background with direct operational law experience advising commanders on LOAC, rules of engagement (ROE), targeting, or detention.
  • Demonstrated, hands-on application of distinction, proportionality, and precautions in real-world operations.
  • Expertise with Geneva Conventions, Additional Protocols, customary international humanitarian law, and relevant ROE frameworks.
  • Service at a command, combatant command legal office, or targeting cell (US or allied forces).
  • Experience leading reviews, teaching, or providing legal advisory services in operational contexts.
  • Exceptional legal writing and analytical skills with a focus on written and verbal clarity.
  • Exposure to intelligence law, cyber law, or the interplay between IHL and international human rights law is a plus.
  • Prior experience in AI evaluation, wargaming, or governance initiatives is considered an asset.
