Federal Grants Management Specialist

Remote, US only$60–$85 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Draft federal funding announcements and Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs), defining program objectives, eligibility requirements, application instructions, and evaluation criteria.
  • Review and score federal grant applications, assessing eligibility, project merit, budget justifications, and alignment with funding requirements.
  • Prepare formal grant monitoring findings, identifying compliance deficiencies, supporting evidence, applicable regulations, and recommended corrective actions.
  • Review and annotate grant applications, award documents, budgets, and compliance reports to develop high-quality AI training materials.
  • Evaluate AI-generated grants management outputs for regulatory accuracy, procedural correctness, and adherence to federal requirements, including 2 CFR Part 200 (Uniform Guidance).
  • Develop realistic grant management scenarios, reference answers, and evaluations covering pre-award, award, and post-award processes.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Proven professional experience in federal grants management, particularly in GS-1109 positions or equivalent federal, state, or local government grant administration roles.
  • Hands-on experience drafting funding notices, reviewing and scoring applications, and documenting monitoring or audit findings.
  • Strong knowledge of federal grants processes, compliance standards, and reporting requirements.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with an emphasis on clarity and technical accuracy.
  • Commitment to customer service and effective collaboration in a remote environment.
  • Prior experience with AI training, model evaluation, or content review is a plus.
