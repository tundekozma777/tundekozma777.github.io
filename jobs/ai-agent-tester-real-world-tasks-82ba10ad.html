AI Agent Tester - Real-World Tasks

$25–$45 per hourContractor

About the Role

Pay: $25-45/hour Time per task: ~1.5 hours per run (evidence and scoring included) Project kickoff: Already active.

Onboarding: Within 24-48 hours of being selected, you'll receive the offer and complete onboarding. You'll begin tasking as soon as tasks are assigned.

You'll need: An active personal AI assistant account (Plus tier), active Muse and Dots access, a smartphone that can screen record, and a payment card you can use for test purchases that are reimbursed afterward Your tasks: Run everyday errands with two AI agents, approve key steps, screen record, and score the results

AI agents are starting to handle real-life errands: paying bills, booking trips, doing the groceries. We want to know how well they actually do it, from the point of view of areal user. That’s you.

You'll test two AI agents side by side, using your own accounts on your phone, and tell us what worked, what didn't, and how it felt.

Responsibilities

  • Runreal-life scenarios: bills, returns, email follow-ups, bookings, shopping, groceries, travel, family logistics, outings, and forms.
  • Approve or stop the agent at key steps.
  • Screen record every attempt.
  • Scoreeachrun completion, quality, control issues, satisfaction, and on time.
  • Share clear written feedback on the agent experience.

Required Qualifications

  • US-based, with a Bachelor's degree.
  • Someone who already uses AI in daily life.
  • 2-3 of experience, preferably in engineering, business management, years or office/operations.
  • Detail-oriented, great at following instructions and documenting each step.
  • Clear written communication.
  • Generalists welcome, domain experts preferred.
  • Nocoding or AI backqround needed.
