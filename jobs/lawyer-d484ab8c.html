Responsibilities

  • Analyze legal scenarios involving litigation, corporate transactions, and regulatory or compliance matters to identify key legal issues, risks, and relevant legal principles.
  • Review and evaluate AI-generated legal analyses, arguments, contracts, and other legal documents for accuracy, completeness, and sound reasoning.
  • Identify legal errors, unsupported conclusions, missing information, and weaknesses in legal reasoning.
  • Apply professional legal judgment to complex or ambiguous situations, considering relevant laws, facts, and competing interpretations.

Required Qualifications

  • Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree from a U.S. law school.
  • Active bar license in at least one U.S. state or the District of Columbia.
  • More than 3 years of substantive post-J.D. legal practice.**.
  • Strong legal reasoning, issue-spotting, legal writing, and document review skills.
  • Excellent written English communication and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently in a remote contractor environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in any legal practice area, including litigation, corporate, commercial, employment, personal injury, criminal, family, immigration, insurance, intellectual property, healthcare, securities, real estate, tax, privacy, environmental, or regulatory law.
  • Experience reviewing complex legal documents, identifying substantive errors, or evaluating competing legal arguments.
  • Familiarity with legal technology, AI-assisted research, or AI evaluation is a plus, but not required.
