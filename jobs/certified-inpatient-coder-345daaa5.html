Healthcare

Certified Inpatient Coder

United States, Remote$50–$70 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review and analyze inpatient medical records to ensure accurate ICD- 10-CM and ICD-10-PCS coding in alignment with current guidelines and best practices.
  • Review clinical documentation for accuracy, completeness, and coding integrity.
  • Identify principal and secondary diagnoses, sequence codes per the Official Guidelines, and assign Present on Admission (POA) indicators.
  • Validate MS-DRG assignment, including identification of CCs and MCCs, and flag hospital-acquired conditions (HACs) and patient safety indicators.
  • Evaluate AI-generated clinical notes and summaries against real-world documentation and coding standards.
  • Code AI-generated diagnostic and procedural information from the record using ICD-10-CM and ICD-10-PCS classification systems.
  • Identify and resolve discrepancies in diagnosis and procedure coding, ensuring the integrity of record abstraction and data capture.
  • Provide clear written feedback on coding errors and complex cases to improve model performance.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Active certification in one of the following: CIC or CCS, or other coding certification through AHIMA and AAPC (e.g., RHIA, RHIT, COC).
  • Inpatient ICD-I0-CM/PCS coding experience.
  • Currently working in inpatient medical coding, clinical documentation improvement, or a closely related documentation role.
  • Availability of at least 10 hours per week.
