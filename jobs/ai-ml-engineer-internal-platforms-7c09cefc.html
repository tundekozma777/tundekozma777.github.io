About the Role

Job Title: AI/ML Engineer, Internal Platforms

Join our team to build and improve a next-generation AI recruiting agent. You'll develop new capabilities, experiment with the latest LLMs and agent frameworks, and ship production-ready AI features that make the agent smarter, more reliable, and more useful.

Responsibilities

  • Build and improve AI agent capabilities using modern LLMs.
  • Develop backend services and APIs in Python.
  • Design and iterate on prompts, workflows, RAG, and agent orchestration.
  • Evaluate model performance and continuously improve quality through experimentation.
  • Collaborate with product and engineering teams to deliver AI-powered features.
  • Deploy and maintain scalable AI systems in production.
  • Stay current with the latest AI models, tools, and best practices.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong Python software engineering skills.
  • Experience building applications with LLMs and AI APIs.
  • Familiarity with agent frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, or Llamalndex.
  • Experience building APIs with FastAPI or similar frameworks.
  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
  • Strong problem-solving skills and an experimental mindset.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience shipping AI products or AI agents to production.
  • Familiarity with RAG, tool calling, model evaluation, or prompt engineering.
  • Contributions to open-source AI projects or personal AI applications.
  • Experience working in fast-paced, AI-first environments.
