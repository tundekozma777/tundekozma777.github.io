Paralegal (General)

Remote$50–$70 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Conduct comprehensive legal research and analysis on a variety of legal topics and case materials.
  • Review, annotate, and summarize legal documents, case files, pleadings, and filings for data training purposes.
  • Leverage eDiscovery platforms, including Relativity, to identify, categorize, and extract relevant information from large data sets.
  • Prepare detailed legal annotations and structured datasets to support machine learning initiatives.
  • Assess and verify the accuracy, completeness, and quality of legal data entries and annotations.
  • Collaborate remotely with project coordinators to meet deliverable standards and project milestones.
  • Provide insight into legal processes, terminology, and procedural nuances to enhance AI system understanding.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Substantial experience as a paralegal within a US-based law firm or legal service provider.
  • Demonstrated proficiency with legal research, document review, and drafting pleadings and filings.
  • Hands-on familiarity with eDiscovery tools, particularly Relativity.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication abilities for precise documentation and feedback.
  • Strong organizational skills for management of large volumes of complex legal data.
  • Attention to detail, accuracy, and reliability in handling sensitive legal information.
  • Interest in or curiosity about the intersection of law and technology; prior AI or data annotation experience is a plus, but not required.
