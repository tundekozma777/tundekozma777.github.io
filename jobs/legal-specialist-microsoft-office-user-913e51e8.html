Legal Specialist (Microsoft Office User)

Remote$25–$40 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Design realistic, multi-step legal prompts that reflect true industry workflows, such as contract review and drafting, legal research, case analysis, discovery support, regulatory compliance assessments, and client advisory memos.
  • Write, review, and refine legal content used for AI training and model evaluation, ensuring accuracy, sound legal reasoning, and professional standards.
  • Help build and score legal evaluations that test how well AI can analyze statutes, case law, contracts, and fact patterns, and identify issues, risks, and appropriate conclusions.
  • Use Microsoft 365 tools, including Copilot, to produce legal deliverables and documentation such as memos, briefs, redlines, summaries, and trackers.
  • Analyze legal materials and AI outputs, and turn findings into clear, actionable recommendations for both legal work product quality and AI improvement.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Proven professional experience in a legal role (e.g., attorney, paralegal, legal counsel, contracts manager, compliance specialist, or legal operations), in practice areas such as corporate, litigation, intellectual property, employment, regulatory, or real estate.
  • Active bar admission, paralegal certification, or equivalent legal credentials (preferred, depending on role).
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft 365, with hands-on experience in Word (including track changes and comparison tools), Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, and Copilot.
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication, including the ability to explain legal concepts clearly to both legal and non-legal audiences.
  • Strong attention to detail, analytical rigor, and the ability to give clear, consistent feedback.
  • Experience with AI training, data annotation, or evaluating AI-generated content.
