Media, Publishing & Communications Law Attorney

Remote — open worldwide, provided you hold an active U.S. bar$140–$400 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Contribute expert-level legal reasoning on media, publishing, and communications law questions, including rights clearance, licensing, and regulatory compliance.
  • Work through realistic scenarios drawn from content licensing, broadcast regulation, and pre-publication review practice, explaining the analysis behind your conclusions.
  • Identify where a question is unsettled, jurisdictionally contested, or turns on facts that change the answer.
  • Review and validate work from other contributors for legal accuracy and clarity of reasoning.
  • Document your reasoning clearly, so the analysis is as useful as the conclusion.
  • Flag ambiguous or poorly framed scenarios to project coordinators instead of resolving them silently.
  • Deliver assigned work on schedule and at the required quality level.

Required Qualifications

  • J.D. and active U.S. bar admission in good standing.
  • 5+ years of substantive media or communications law practice — hands-on, not supervisory-only or adjacent exposure.
  • Acorporate law foundation, ideally built at a Vault 100 firm.
