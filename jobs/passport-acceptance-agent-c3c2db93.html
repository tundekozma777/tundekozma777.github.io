Passport Acceptance Agent

Remote, United States$30–$40 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review sample passport questions and flag any that don't sound like what real applicants ask.
  • Write rubrics that define a correct, complete answer on new applications, renewals, and replacements, including forms, fees, and processing options.
  • Cover identity and citizenship documents, photo requirements, and applications for minors in your rubrics.
  • Evaluate AI-generated answers against your rubrics, flagging errors, omissions, and outdated guidance.
  • Share clear written feedback with the project team, working asynchronously.

Required Qualifications

  • Current or recent passport acceptance agent designation (post office, county clerk, library, or similar).
  • 1+ years helping the public with passport applications.
  • Working knowledge of forms, fees, ID and citizenship documents, and rules for minors.
  • US-based.
  • Able to start quickly and commit 20-30 hours/week.
  • Clear, precise writing.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with lost, stolen, or damaged passports and name changes.
  • Experience creating guides, checklists, or training materials.
  • Experience helping a diverse range of applicants, including rural and non-English-speaking customers.
