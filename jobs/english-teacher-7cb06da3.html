English Teacher

United States only$15–$40 per hourContractor, Remote

Responsibilities

  • Complete realistic teaching-related digital tasks (emailing parents and students, scheduling conferences, booking field trips to theaters or author events, coordinating with colleagues) using AI assistant tools under standardized, repeatable conditions.
  • Read detailed task instructions and grading guidelines closely and follow them precisely and consistently, including multi-step requirements and fine details.
  • Give an AI assistant clear instructions for real-world tasks, then review its work for accuracy, tone, grammar, completeness, and whether it followed the instructions given.
  • Apply detailed grading guidelines to evaluate, rank, and annotate assistant responses, with a focus on language quality, literary and stylistic appropriateness, clarity, and tone.
  • Draw on your knowledge of literature, rhetoric, and composition to judge whether AI-written text is well-constructed, audience-appropriate, and faithful to the request.
  • Use AI training tools and platforms to record findings and submit evaluation data in line with project standards and milestones.

Required Qualifications

  • Based in the US, with an iPhone with iMessage and a Facebook and/or Instagram account.
  • Bachelor's degree in English, literature, creative writing, linguistics, comparative literature, education, or a related humanities field.
  • Strong instruction-following skills: the ability to read detailed, multi-step guidelines carefully and apply every requirement accurately, without skipping or reinterpreting steps.
  • Experience teaching English or literature (for example, language arts, literature, composition, creative writing, or ESL) at the K-12, college, or adult education level.
  • Solid knowledge of literary analysis, writing conventions, and grammar, with the ability to evaluate text for style, tone, and clarity.
  • Daily, hands-on use of Google Classroom to run your classes: posting assignments and announcements, giving feedback on student work, managing due dates, and communicating with students and parents, alongside regular use of Gmail and Google Calendar.
  • Experience using AI tools for real, practical tasks, with comfort giving an AI assistant instructions and checking its work.
  • Exceptional attention to detail in grammar, tone, and accuracy when reviewing AI-generated writing.
