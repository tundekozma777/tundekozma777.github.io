Codeforces Problem Setter

Remote$45–$65 per hour (listed range, task-based pay)Contractor (15hrs+ a week)

About the Role

Pay: $30 - $100

Responsibilities

  • Design original competitive programming problems, absent from Codeforces, AtCoder, and all other judges.
  • Write a correct reference solution and verify it against the constraints.
  • Build a robust test suite: edge cases, maximum constraints, and tests that break plausible wrong approaches.
  • Implement checkers for problems with multiple valid outputs.
  • Document the problem’s intended solution, complexity, and the reasoning behind your test cases.
  • Review problems from other contributors for correctness and test coverage.
  • Flag problems that turn out to be ambiguous or have unintended solutions.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong competitive programming record — Codeforces, AtCoder, ICPC, IOI, or equivalent.
  • Advanced C++ for reference solutions and checkers.
  • Deep algorithmic knowledge and the ability to reason about constraints, complexity, and edge cases.
  • Meticulous attention to detail in validation and output consistency.
  • Clear written and verbal English for documenting problems and edge cases.
  • Comfortable working independently and meeting deadlines.
  • Prior experience as a problem setter, tester, or checker on a major platform is a strong plus.

Engagement Details

  • Compensation is output-based; experts are paid per task that meets the project specifications.
  • The time required may vary depending on your experience and workflow. Minimum submission requirements apply.
