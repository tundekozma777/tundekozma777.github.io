AI Trainer (Life Sciences)

Global, fully remote$60–$120 per hour (listed range, task-based pay)Contractor

Responsibilities

  • Create realistic computational life-science tasks that challenge advanced AI models.

Required Qualifications

  • Prior paid experience training, evaluating, benchmarking, or improving AI systems on scientific or technical tasks.
  • Strong practical proficiency with Python.
  • Deep expertise in at least one relevant life-science specialization.
  • Hands-on experience using computational methods for modeling, simulation, bioinformatics, biomedical analysis, statistics, or reproducible research.
  • Relevant specializations may include biology, computational biology, bioinformatics, medicine and health, biomedical science, neuroscience, genomics, ecology, and evolution.
  • Peer-reviewed publications or preprints involving computational methods Public research code or bioinformatics pipelines Google Scholar, ORCID, or equivalent research profiles PhysioNet or other biomedical competition results Open-source scientific software contributions Public datasets, models, notebooks, or technical projects.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience creating AI-training tasks, reference answers, rubrics, graders, or benchmark problems.
  • Experience reviewing other AI trainers’ technical submissions.
  • Experience with NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Biopython, PyTorch, JAX, or comparable scientific tools.
  • Prior experience with long-horizon or agentic AI evaluations.
  • Availability to begin immediately.

Engagement Details

  • Compensation is output-based. Experts are paid for tasks that meet project specifications and quality requirements. Task availability and completion time may vary.

Application Process

  • Submit your application, AI-training experience, specialization, and public technical evidence.
