Senior Vehicle Damage and Condition Expert

Global-Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor (~10 hours per week)

Responsibilities

  • Review and analyze comprehensive vehicle evidence such as damage photos, repair estimates, condition reports, and collision scene footage.
  • Write detailed, professional-grade damage estimates and condition assessments, applying the standards expected of a licensed appraiser or ACTAR-certified reconstructionist.
  • Conduct remote accident quality audits and causation analysis, clearly documenting rationale and supporting evidence.
  • Evaluate and grade vehicle condition using established criteria common in fleet, auction, or OEM inspection protocols.
  • Deliver well-structured written explanations and verbal feedback that communicate findings with clarity and authority.
  • Collaborate with a project team to uphold consistency and quality in all deliverables submitted to the platform.
  • Adapt review methods to reflect current industry practices and maintain compliance with project guidelines.

Preferred Qualifications

  • 5+ years of professional experience in vehicle damage appraisal, automotive claims, collision repair assessment, or fleet/auction inspection.
  • Expertise in auto damage estimating, vehicle inspection, and accident quality auditing.
  • Background as an auto damage appraiser, ACTAR-certified reconstructionist, OEM auditor, or fleet/auction inspector strongly preferred.
  • State adjuster license, I-CAR, or ACTAR certification considered a plus.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with a focus on thorough documentation and reasoned justifications.
  • Strong analytical and critical thinking abilities, with an aptitude for objective evidence review and reporting.
  • Comfortable engaging in a remote, flexible, and independent expert contributor opportunity.
