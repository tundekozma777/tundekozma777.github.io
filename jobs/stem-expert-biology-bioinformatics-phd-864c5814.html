STEM Expert – Biology & Bioinformatics (PhD)

Remote(Worldwide)$45–$90 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Design, evaluate, and critique biology, bioinformatics, and computational biology workflows to support AI benchmarking initiatives.
  • Provide expert-level insights on genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic, and other biological data analysis pipelines.
  • Assess and optimize pipeline parameters, tool configurations, and statistical methods for accuracy and reproducibility.
  • Analyze biological datasets (e.g., NGS, RNA-seq, single-cell, variant calling, protein structure) and provide actionable recommendations.
  • Leverage domain knowledge to guide the development and validation of biology-related AI models and tools.
  • Communicate complex scientific concepts clearly, both in writing and verbally, to technical and non-technical collaborators.
  • Document findings, methodologies, and best practices to ensure knowledge transfer across the customer’s team.
  • Areas of Expertise (we welcome applicants from any of the following):.
  • Genomics & NGS analysis Transcriptomics & single-cell analysis Proteomics & structural biology Molecular & cell biology Systems biology & biological network analysis Drug discovery & computational pharmacology Biostatistics & machine learning applied to biology.

Required Qualifications

  • PhD in biology, bioinformatics, computational biology, genomics, or a closely related STEM field, or equivalent industry/research experience.
  • Professional written and spoken English (C1 or higher).
  • Deep expertise in at least one of the areas listed above, with hands-on experience analyzing real biological data.
  • Proficiency in Python and/or R for scientific analysis, plus experience with standard tools in your subfield (e.g., BLAST, samtools, GATK, Bioconductor, Biopython).
  • Strong statistical, quantitative, and analytical reasoning skills.
  • Outstanding written and verbal communication abilities, with the ability to clearly document and explain technical concepts.
  • Track record of rigorous scientific problem-solving in complex biological domains.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience building reproducible workflows (Nextflow, Snakemake) and working in Linux/command-line environments.
  • Experience with AI/ML-assisted biology workflows or benchmarking.
  • Peer-reviewed publications, patents, or contributions to open-source bioinformatics software.
