Veterans Service Officer

Remote, United States$40–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review sample veterans benefits questions and flag any that don’t sound like what real veterans or family members ask.
  • Write rubrics that define a correct, complete answer on service-connected disability compensation, including filing a claim and the evidence involved.
  • Cover dependents and survivor benefits (such as DIC and CHAMPVA) and appeals and decision reviews in your rubrics.
  • Evaluate AI-generated answers against your rubrics, flagging errors, omissions, and outdated guidance.
  • Share clear written feedback with the project team, working asynchronously.

Required Qualifications

  • Current VA accreditation (VSO representative, County Veterans Service Officer, accredited claims agent, or similar).
  • 1+ years preparing and filing VA claims for veterans or their families.
  • Working knowledge of disability compensation, dependents and survivor benefits, and appeals.
  • US-based.
  • Able to start quickly and commit 20-30 hours/week.
  • Clear, precise writing.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with military records such as the DD-214.
  • Experience creating guides, checklists, or training materials.
  • Experience helping a diverse range of veterans and families, including rural and non-English-speaking clients.
