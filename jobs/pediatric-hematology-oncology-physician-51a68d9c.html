Pediatric Hematology/Oncology Physician

Remote, United States only$160–$180 per hourContractor

About the Role

In this role, you'll use your clinical expertise to evaluate AI-generated transcripts and medical notes from outpatient pediatric hematology/oncology encounters. Your feedback will help improve the accuracy, completeness, and clinical relevance of AI-assisted documentation.

Responsibilities

  • Review AI-generated transcripts of outpatient pediatric hematology/oncology visits for clinical accuracy, completeness, and clarity.
  • Evaluate clinical documentation, including History of Present Illness (HPI), interval histories, and Assessment and Plans (A&Ps).
  • Identify inaccuracies or missing details related to chemotherapy regimens, treatment toxicities, supportive care, and laboratory findings.
  • Assess pediatric-specific considerations, including weight-and BSA-based dosing, developmental milestones, and age-appropriate communication.
  • Review how documentation captures caregiver involvement, shared decision-making, and psychosocial considerations.
  • Provide clear, actionable feedback to improve AI-generated clinical notes and EHR documentation.

Required Qualifications

  • MD, DO, or equivalent medical degree, with board certification or eligibility in Pediatric Hematology/Oncology.
  • At least 2 years of recent outpatient pediatric hematology/oncology experience following fellowship.
  • Strong experience managing chemotherapy, longitudinal patient care, and treatment-related complications.
  • Proven ability to independently document HPIs, interval histories, and Assessment and Plans.
  • Solid understanding of pediatric chemotherapy protocols, transfusion practices, supportive care, and age-specific laboratory interpretation.
  • Strong written communication skills and attention to clinical detail.
  • Based in the United States.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience treating both malignant and non-malignant pediatric hematologic conditions, such as sickle cell disease, hemophilia, and ITP.
  • Familiarity with AI scribe technology, EHR documentation templates, medical chart review, or clinical annotation projects.

Application Process

  • Candidates will complete an AI interview to assess their relevant clinical expertise and experience.
