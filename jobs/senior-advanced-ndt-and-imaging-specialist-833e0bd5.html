Senior Advanced NDT and Imaging Specialist

Global-Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor (~10 hours per week)

Responsibilities

  • Review and interpret diverse NDT data sets, including industrial CT volumes, X-ray images, ultrasonic scans, shearography data, and fiber optic inspection records.
  • Provide detailed written assessments and dispositions for inspection records, explaining findings and the rationale behind each decision.
  • Deliver feedback on imaging quality, detection limits, and potential false positives or negatives in complex inspection scenarios.
  • Apply standards knowledge (ASNT, NAS 410, EN 4179) to ensure evaluations align with industry best practices and regulatory compliance.
  • Document expert recommendations and insights to support annotation, labeling, and model development tasks.
  • Collaborate asynchronously through clear written and verbal communications to refine project deliverables and address technical queries.

Preferred Qualifications

  • 7+ years of advanced experience in NDT, with Level Il or Level Ill certifications in industrial CT, X-ray, ultrasonic, shearography, or fiber optic inspection,.
  • Proven track record interpreting real-world inspection data in industries such as aerospace, nuclear, automotive, battery, composites, or tire manufacturing,.
  • ASNT, NAS 410, or EN 4179 certification (required).
  • Expertise in specialist imaging modalities, including EPRI-qualified nuclear UT analysis, composite and battery inspection, or tire NDT.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills for documenting findings and collaborating remotely.
  • Attention to detail and a methodical approach to technical evaluations and reporting.
  • Familiarity with digital pletforms or remote collaboration tools a plus.
