Senior Data Analyst

Remote, US Based only$50–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review data sets for Pll, flagging sensitive information and following data privacy best practices.
  • Review and assess AI responses to data and business analysis scenarios, providing expert feedback to improve accuracy and precision.
  • Apply QA practices to validate data quality, flag inconsistencies, and verify the correctness of analytical results.
  • Create objective evaluation frameworks and grading criteria to assess AI performance on analytical tasks.
  • Collaborate with product and research teams to refine data, guidelines, and best practices for AI-driven analytics.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in business, analytics, statistics, information systems, or arelated field (or equivalent professional experience).
  • 3+ years of experience as a Business Analyst or Data Analyst working with large business data sets.
  • Hands-on experience with QA processes, such as data validation, reconciliation, and verifying analytical outputs.
  • Demonstrated expertise in handling and analyzing large business data sets.
  • Proven experience managing data containing personally identifiable information (Pll) and sensitive material.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Familiarity or experience working with Pll, including data privacy and handling best practices.
  • Proficiency with SQL, Excel, and BI or visualization tools.
  • Advanced degree or professional certification in business analysis or data analytics.
  • Prior exposure to AI, data labeling, or model evaluation initiatives.
