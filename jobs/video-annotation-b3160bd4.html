Video Annotation

Remote$15–$25 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Carefully review and annotate video content based on detailed guidelines and rubrics provided for the project.
  • Apply consistent tagging, labeling, or classification to video segments, ensuring high precision and adherence to annotation standards.
  • Document and communicate annotation decisions effectively through written and verbal channels.
  • Utilize annotation tools or platforms, adapting quickly to new interfaces as required by the customer.
  • Collaborate with project leads or quality assurance professionals to clarify requirements and refine outputs.
  • Submit annotations within agreed-upon milestones, maintaining a focus on accuracy and completeness.
  • Provide feedback on guidelines or processes to enhance annotation quality and efficiency.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Demonstrated experience with video annotation and a strong: understanding of annotation rubrics.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with an emphasis on clarity and detail orientation.
  • Ability to follow detailed instructions and adapt quickly to evolving project requirements.
  • Experience working on data annotation, labeling, or similar projects — generalists with relevant experience are welcome.
  • Proficiency with digital annotation tools or related software (no video editing experience needed).
  • Proactive in seeking clarification and providing constructive feedback on processes.
  • Commitment to delivering high-quality, accurate annotations within project timelines.
