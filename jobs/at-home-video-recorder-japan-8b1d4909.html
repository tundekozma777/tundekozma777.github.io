At Home Video Recorder (Japan)

$12–$21 per hourContractor

About the Role

Help train the next generation of personal robotics. You'll capture high-quality video demonstrations of everyday household tasks using a UMI gripper, a wearable device that mimics a robot's hand while letting you perform tasks naturally. Your demonstrations become training data that teaches robots how to operate in real homes.

Responsibilities

  • Capture and submit high-quality, specification-compliant video demonstrations of everyday household tasks using a UMI gripper wearable device.
  • Follow detailed, task-specific instructions to ensure all recordings meet rigorous project standards.
  • Review feedback from assigned reviewers and iteratively refine your approach to improve data quality.
  • Operate and set up recording equipment and the UMI gripper to accurately mimic real-world hand movements while performing tasks.
  • Maintain a consistent weekly workflow to meet the minimum approved data requirements.

Required Qualifications

  • Keen attention to detail and demonstrated ability to follow complex specifications precisely.
  • Manual dexterity and experience with physical task precision in varied household environments.
  • Comfort with operating, wearing, and setting up specialized devices and recording equipment.
  • Capacity to incorporate feedback and make iterative improvements to processes and outputs.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, including openness to prompt feedback exchanges.
  • Reliable access to a fully equipped Japanese household environment (kitchen, living spaces, and common items).
  • Ability to upload large video files via stable, high-speed internet from your home environment.
  • This role involves prolonged use of a VR headset; candidates should be comfortable with extended wear as part of their regular work environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Genuine curiosity about personal robotics, AI, and emerging technology in home environments.
  • Prior experience participating in data collection, usability studies, or similar hands-on projects.
  • Patience for repetitive, precision-oriented work and willingness to adapt as project needs evolve.

Engagement Details

  • Start at a standard rate of $12/hour, with the opportunity to increase your effective hourly earnings to $21/hour as you become more efficient.
  • Compensation is based on completed video hours, so the faster you work while maintaining quality, the higher your effective hourly rate!
