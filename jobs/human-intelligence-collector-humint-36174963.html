Human Intelligence Collector (HUMINT)

Remote, US-based preferred$80–$170 per hourContractor (at least ~15 hs a week)

Responsibilities

  • Conduct or assess structured elicitation and interview sessions to extract accurate and relevant information from varied subjects.
  • Establish rapport with participants to encourage open communication without applying pressure or coercion.
  • Analyze responses in real-time, reading tone, pacing, and nonverbal cues, and dynamically adjusting questioning strategies.
  • Assess the internal consistency, reliability, and potential evasiveness of responses, providing confidence assessments for each interaction.
  • Identify and annotate emotional and motivational drivers influencing participants’ responses.
  • Document your analytical reasoning in a clear, traceable manner to support downstream review and learning.
  • Score or annotate sessions according to a detailed rubric, maintaining objectivity and consistency across multiple cases.

Preferred Qualifications

  • 3-5+ years of experience in professional interviewing, intelligence elicitation, or investigative questioning (e.g., military HUMINT, law-enforcement interviewing, skilled negotiation).
  • Training in recognized interviewing methodologies such as the PEACE model, cognitive interviewing, or similar frameworks.
  • Expertise in real-time interpretation of paralinguistic and nonverbal cues (tone, pacing, hesitation, gestures).
  • Demonstrated ability to clearly justify analytical reads in writing, not just by intuition.
  • Exceptional written English skills with a focus on clarity, precision, and defensibility.
  • Experience working with diverse populations or through interpreters.
