Responsibilities

  • Contribute expert-level legal reasoning on energy and utilities regulatory questions, including rate design, tariff structure, and compliance obligations.
  • Work through realistic regulatory scenarios drawn from FERC, state PUC, and ISO/RTO practice, explaining the analysis behind your conclusions.
  • Identify where a question is unsettled, jurisdictionally contested, or turns on facts that change the answer.
  • Review and validate work from other contributors for legal accuracy and clarity of reasoning.
  • Document your reasoning clearly, so the analysis is as useful as the conclusion.
  • Flag ambiguous or poorly framed scenarios to project coordinators instead of resolving them silently.
  • Deliver assigned work on schedule and at the required quality level.

  • 4+ years of substantive energy and utilities regulatory practice — hands-on, not supervisory-only or adjacent exposure.
  • Acorporate law foundation, ideally built at a Vault 100 firm.
