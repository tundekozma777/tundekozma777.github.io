MBSE Engineer - SysML V2

RemoteContractor$55–$120 per hour (advertised range)

About the Role

Create reference SysML v2 models and assess AI-generated systems models for technical accuracy, fidelity, and traceability.

Responsibilities

  • Translate engineering requirements into SysML v2 models and build reference architectures for evaluation.
  • Create, review, and validate models with suitable systems modeling tools.
  • Develop assessment rubrics and provide structured feedback on AI-generated models.
  • Review Concepts of Operations, Interface Control Documents, and architecture specifications.
  • Collaborate asynchronously to clarify deliverables and resolve technical questions.

Required Qualifications

  • Professional systems engineering experience with hands-on SysML v1 and some exposure to v2, or demonstrated experience modeling in SysML v2.
  • Ability to turn engineering requirements into accurate models and apply MBSE methods using systems modeling tools.
  • Understanding of system architecture, requirements traceability, and model-based systems engineering principles.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Cameo Systems Modeler, MagicDraw, or comparable tools and graphical or textual SysML v2 environments.
  • Knowledge of interface control, ConOps, technical writing, and asynchronous collaboration.
  • Experience in complex engineering domains such as aerospace, defense, automotive, maritime, space, or communications.
  • Familiarity with model verification, technical reviews, rubric development, or AI and data-driven engineering.

Engagement Details

The project pays per task. Hours vary with project needs, with an estimated commitment of 15 hours per week. The initial pilot is expected to run for approximately four weeks to three months.
