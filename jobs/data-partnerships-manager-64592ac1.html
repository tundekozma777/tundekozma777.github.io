About the Role

Job Title: Data Partnerships Manager

We are looking for a Data Partnerships Manager to identify, engage, and close partnerships with companies interested in contributing workflow data for AI training and evaluation.

You will own the partnership process end-to-end: sourcing companies, running discovery conversations, understanding business workflows, navigating privacy and security discussions, negotiating commercial terms, and onboarding new partners.

Responsibilities

  • Source and engage companies with valuable operational workflows across a variety of industries.
  • Build relationships with founders, executives, and operational leaders.
  • Conduct discovery calls to understand business processes, workflow complexity, and partnership potential.
  • Educate prospective partners on how workflow data is used to train and evaluate AI systems.
  • Lead conversations around privacy, security, anonymization, data ownership, and compliance requirements.
  • Design partnership structures that align company goals with AI customer needs.
  • Negotiate commercial terms and close partnership agreements.
  • Build and manage a pipeline of high-quality partnership opportunities.
  • Collaborate with internal operations, product, and data teams to successfully onboard new partners.
  • Help develop repeatable acquisition strategies, messaging, and partnership playbooks as the program scales.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in Business Development, Partnerships, Enterprise Sales, Strategic Partnerships, or a similar customer-facing role.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to build credibility with senior decision-makers.
  • Comfortable discussing sensitive topics including privacy, security, confidentiality, and data ownership.
  • Ability to explain technical concepts in a clear and compelling way to non-technical audiences.
  • Strong discovery, qualification, and consultative selling skills.
  • Experience with outbound prospecting and pipeline development.
  • Proficiency with tools such as LinkedIn Sales Navigator, HubSpot or similar platforms.
  • Highly resourceful, execution-oriented, and comfortable operating in fast-moving environments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in AI, data, SaaS, marketplaces, or technology partnerships.
  • Experience working with SMBs, founders, and owner-operated businesses.
  • Familiarity with data licensing, data acquisition, or emerging AI infrastructure markets.
