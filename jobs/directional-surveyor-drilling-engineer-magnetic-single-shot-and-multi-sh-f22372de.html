Directional Surveyor / Drilling Engineer (Magnetic Single-Shot and Multi-Shot Film)

Remote$20–$60 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Analyze and interpret magnetic single-shot and multi-shot survey film data, including traditional film-based survey discs.
  • Conduct quality control checks on wellbore survey data, identifying errors, inconsistencies, and interference indicators.
  • Apply calculations for declination, magnetic interference corrections, and closure, ensuring high-accuracy wellbore mapping.
  • Document step-by-step reasoning used in survey interpretation and corrections for educational and reference purposes.
  • Evaluate reported wellbore positions for accuracy and recommend corrective actions where necessary.
  • Contribute real-world scenarios and complex cases involving magnetic survey tools and film-based measurements.
  • Provide clear written and verbal explanations of techniques, findings, and methodologies.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Extensive hands-on experience with magnetic single-shot and multi-shot survey instruments, especially those using film-based technology.
  • Strong background in directional surveying, survey analysis, and drilling engineering within oil & gas or related sectors.
  • Demonstrated ability to perform complex survey calculations, including magnetic declination, interference detection, and closure computations.
  • Expertise in identifying, documenting, and correcting survey data errors and magnetic influences.
  • Proficiency in written and verbal communication, with the ability to explain technical concepts to both experts and non-specialists.
  • Familiarity with standard industry practices for wellbore survey QC and directional drilling fundamentals.
  • Detail-oriented, analytical, and committed to delivering high-quality, real-world input to support AI training initiatives.
