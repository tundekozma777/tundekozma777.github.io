Military Writing Specialist

Remote (US Only)$40–$80 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Create realistic military and federal status reports, summaries, and executive updates based on prompts and supplied artifacts.
  • Develop “golden responses” that demonstrate high-quality, standards-aligned reporting for use in AI model training.
  • Draft grading rubrics and guidelines that reflect best practices in federal and military writing assessment.
  • Evaluate AI-generated reports, providing detailed feedback in both written and verbal form.
  • Share context on stylistic differences and individual approaches to common military documents (e.g., WARs, SITREPs, AARs, executive summaries).
  • Produce supporting documents such as decision briefs, memoranda, and action officer updates when required.
  • Collaborate with other subject matter experts to ensure diversity of reporting styles and adherence to branch-specific terminology.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Former U.S. Department of War (Department of Defense) professional with at least 3 years of relevant experience.
  • Experience producing templated and standard reports such as Weekly Activity Reports (WARs), Situation Reports (SITREPs), After-Action Reviews, or similar artifacts.
  • Expertise spanning two or more branches of the U.S. military is a plus; a diverse background across roles is highly valued.
  • Proven proficiency in written and verbal communication, particularly in conveying technical or operational subject matter.
  • Demonstrated ability to craft, grade, and review back office documents, PowerPoints, memos, and spreadsheets for military or federal purposes.
  • US-based contributor with broad familiarity with federal documentation standards and compliance best practices.
  • Comfortable collaborating remotely and sharing insights on nuanced, highly procedural reporting tasks.
