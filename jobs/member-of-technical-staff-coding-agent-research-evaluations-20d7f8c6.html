Member of Technical Staff, Coding Agent Research & Evaluations

About the Role

Design benchmarks and data systems to measure how coding agents perform on real software engineering tasks.

Responsibilities

  • Define coding-agent benchmarks, scoring methods, rubrics, and quality standards.
  • Lead research on coding model performance and create datasets, reference examples, and evaluation protocols.
  • Investigate recurring model failures and use the findings to improve training and evaluation.
  • Build infrastructure for large-scale experiments, data generation, review, and evaluation.
  • Work with researchers and engineers to make evaluations reproducible and report findings in benchmark studies and technical reports.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong software engineering skills in Python, C++, or a comparable language.
  • At least three years of experience in software engineering, ML, AI research, evaluation, or a related technical area.
  • Experience designing or validating technical assessments, benchmarks, coding tasks, or evaluation methods.
  • Familiarity with LLMs, coding agents, reinforcement learning, or model evaluation.
  • Ability to automate workflows, investigate complex model behavior, communicate findings clearly, and work with changing research priorities.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with frontier AI, coding agents, or model evaluation research.
  • Experience independently leading research projects or designing benchmarks and datasets at scale.
  • Familiarity with agentic workflows, tool use, reinforcement learning, or post-training.
  • Publications, open-source contributions, or technical leadership in AI, ML, or software engineering.
