Intelligence Agency Officer

Remote$80–$170 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Analyze and interpret complex intelligence data, providing nuanced feedback for AI training scenarios.
  • Review, evaluate, and enhance written content and communications reflecting intelligence operations and methodologies.
  • Create realistic, scenario-based materials and operational narratives representative of intelligence missions.
  • Offer expert-level insights on structured and unstructured data, identifying trends and detecting anomalies relevant to intelligence contexts.

  • Current or previous professional experience within a civilian intelligence agency, such as the CIA, NSA, MI6/SIS, GCHQ, or an equivalent national intelligence organization.
  • Demonstrated expertise in intelligence-gathering methodologies, threat analysis, and operational planning.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with a keen attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to synthesize large volumes of information and present actionable, concise insights.
  • Familiarity with various intelligence disciplines (HUMINT, SIGINT, OSINT, etc.) and their application to real-world scenarios.
  • Experience producing, reviewing, or training materials for intelligence or analytical audiences.
  • Comfort operating in a distributed, remote engagement with a commitment to high-quality deliverables.
