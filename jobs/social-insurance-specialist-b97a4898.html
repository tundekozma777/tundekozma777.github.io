Social Insurance Specialist

United States, Remote$60–$85 per hourContractor

Responsibilities

  • Review and analyze AI-generated social insurance benefit claims, ensuring comprehensive evaluation of evidence and adherence to established statutory and regulatory guidelines.
  • Assess AI-generated eligibility and entitlement determinations for retirement, survivors, disability, and supplemental income programs, weighing complex evidence accurately.
  • Complete detailed benefit determinations and computations, and document rationales with clarity and precision for AI training.
  • Conduct quality assurance reviews of AI-generated claims decisions, pinpointing inconsistencies or errors according to agency policy and program standards (e.g., POMS).
  • Utilize specialized claims and records systems to access, update, and manage claim files and supporting documentation.
  • Simulate higher-level review decisions, providing expertise on reconsiderations, appeals, and overpayment or underpayment processes for AI training.
  • Deliver constructive written and verbal feedback to enhance AI model training accuracy and effectiveness.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Proven expertise as a Social Insurance Specialist or equivalent, with strong experience in eligibility determination, benefit entitlement, and evidence gathering.
  • Familiarity with GS-0105: Social Insurance Administration functions, or related series such as GS-0996: Veterans Claims Examining and GS-0512: Internal Revenue Agent.
  • Demonstrated skill evaluating benefit claims and applying program rules in a regulated environment.
  • Experience using claims processing and case management systems, such as SSA’s MCS and MSSICS, or similar platforms.
  • Background in quality assurance for claims or similar processes within federal, state, or related agencies.
  • Ability to synthesize complex information and present informed decisions for higher-level review processes.
